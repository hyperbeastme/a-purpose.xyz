Eternal Bonds

Senior Thesis · 8 Looks · 2025

Concept

Parasitism, like tumors, invades the human body, creating a relentless struggle between survival and destruction. These forces reflect the deeply personal journey I experienced with my father, becoming a powerful metaphor for our bond. Circles, symbolizing eternity, are woven into the designs as a reminder that our connection remains unbroken, even through life’s most painful moments.

Replace these blocks with your final photography. Mixed proportions preserve the mood-board rhythm without forcing every image into the same crop.
