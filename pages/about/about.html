Darrius Parker

fashion designer / interdisciplinary artist

a fashion designer from St. Louis, MO, with a background in fine art and a BFA in Fashion Design from Columbia College Chicago.

His practice explores identity, memory, emotion, and human connection through patternmaking, material experimentation, and garment construction. Working between technical precision and conceptual design, he develops garments that challenge conventional notions of form while remaining rooted in craftsmanship.

Drawing from personal experience, He approaches clothing as a medium for narrative rather than decoration. Through silhouette, texture, and deconstruction, his work examines the relationship between the body, memory, and the lasting impact of lived experience.
